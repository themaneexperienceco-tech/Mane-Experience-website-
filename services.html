Before You Arrive

Come As You Are — Just Ready

  • Hair arrives clean — freshly washed and free of product buildup.
  • Hair arrives blow-dried — fully dry from root to end.
  • Hair arrives fully detangled — smooth and snag-free from root to end.

A Note on Preparation

Coming in clean, dry, and detangled protects your appointment time and lets us focus fully on your install. If hair arrives unprepared, additional time and a detangling fee may apply — we'll always let you know before any work begins.
