Before You Arrive

A Note on Preparation

Coming in clean, dry and detangled protects your appointment time. If hair arrives unprepared, additional time and a detangling fee may apply. We'll always let you know before any work begins.

  • Hair arrives clean, free of product buildup.
  • Hair arrives blow-dried, fully dry.
  • Hair arrives fully detangled.
