The Founder

Built on Faith,
Fueled by Purpose

The Mane Experience wasn't born from a simple business plan. It was born from a leap of faith. It is the product of quiet courage, consistent dedication, and a deep belief that purpose is worth pursuing even when the path forward isn't entirely clear.

Creating this brand meant trusting the process in every sense of the word: trusting herself, trusting God, and embracing the beautiful uncertainty that comes with building something meaningful from the ground up.

This is not just a hairstyling business. This is a livelihood, a calling, and a love language, expressed through healthy hair, genuine connection, and the kind of care clients can feel the moment they walk through the door.

“I didn't just build a business. I built a life rooted in faith, purpose, and the belief that I was meant for more.”
The Mission

To Deliver More Than
a Beautiful Style

The goal is simple and sincere: to make every client feel informed, seen, heard, and genuinely cared for. A great hairstyle is just the beginning. The deeper mission is to create confidence, the kind that starts at the chair and follows you out into the world.

Every appointment is an opportunity to build a real relationship, educate clients on the health of their hair, and deliver a luxury-level experience that honors both the craft and the person in the chair.

A Little About Me

The Person Behind the Brand

Beyond the chair, I am someone who finds joy in the small, meaningful things. I love trying new foods, discovering new restaurants, and soaking up different experiences whenever the opportunity arises.

At heart, I am a true homebody. I treasure rest, balance, and the comfort of my own space. You'll often find me watching a great movie or getting lost in a new series.

In person, I'm naturally quiet and a little shy at first, but warm, friendly, and genuinely invested in the people around me. When you sit in my chair, you're not just a client. You are someone whose time, comfort, and beauty matter deeply to me.
