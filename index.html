Before You Arrive

Come As You Are — Just Ready

Arriving prepared protects your appointment time and lets us focus entirely on your install.

  • Hair is  clean  — freshly washed, no buildup.
  • Hair is  blow-dried  — fully dry from root to end.
  • Hair is  fully detangled  — smooth and snag-free.

A Note on Preparation

Arriving with clean, dry, and detangled hair keeps your appointment running smoothly and means every minute is spent on what you came for — your install.

If hair arrives unprepared, additional time and a detangling fee may apply. We'll always let you know before any additional charges are assessed.
